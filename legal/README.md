It does not authorize unrelated modifications, business use, paid distribution or rebranding.

Third-party software retains its own licenses; in particular, LGPL libraries may be modified/replaced, and necessary debugging/relinking rights are preserved by section 5 of the application license.

Bundled library license texts and an inventory are generated under resources/legal before packaging. THIRD_PARTY_SOURCES.md gives upstream source/build locations and replacement guidance. Do not remove these notices from redistributed official files.

These documents express the requested licensing policy and are not a jurisdiction-specific legal opinion. Before a wider distribution or an enforcement action, have a qualified lawyer review ownership, the intended personal-use definition, consumer-law requirements and third-party source obligations. No universal enforceability guarantee is made.
